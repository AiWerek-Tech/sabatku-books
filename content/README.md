# Published book content

This public repository contains approved hosted reading editions in Indonesian and English, including *Kasih di Tengah Kobaran Konflik / Love Under Fire*, the Q4 2026 Indonesian Mission Quarterlies for children and youth/adults, and the 2026 Indonesian *Bacaan Persembahan dan Persepuluhan* booklet. Drafts, internal QA files, and private permission documents stay outside the repository. Each edition's publication, attribution, and offline rights are recorded in its metadata. Mission Quarterlies use separate book works by audience and quarterly editions with dated weekly chapters. The stewardship booklet is one annual edition with a weekly chapter for every Sabbath; Android also bundles the Indonesian chapters and cover for offline reading. A work may also list genuinely provider-hosted editions in its catalog; those entries contain official source links only and never copy provider text. The books catalog is published through GitHub Pages.
